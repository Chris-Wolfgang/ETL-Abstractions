Class EtlPipelineStageProgressExtensions

Per-stage typed progress for EtlPipeline (#382): attach a transformer or loader together with an IProgress<T> that receives that stage's own progress type, the counterpart of WithProgress on the fluent Pipeline builder. The aggregate EtlPipelineProgress passed to RunAsync(IProgress<EtlPipelineProgress>?, CancellationToken) is reported as before; the two are independent.
